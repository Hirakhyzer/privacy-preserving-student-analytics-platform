# Student-Support Boundary

This repository is for independent synthetic research and planning.

It must not be used as:

- student surveillance infrastructure
- automatic grading or discipline tooling
- admissions or scholarship decision software
- immigration, finance, or housing decision tooling
- a substitute for educators, advisors, accessibility teams, counselors, privacy officers, or legal review

Real deployments require notice, consent where applicable, data minimization, access controls, retention controls, fairness validation, accessibility review, human appeal workflows, and local governance.
