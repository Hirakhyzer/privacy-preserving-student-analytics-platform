# Governance and Ethics

The **Privacy-Preserving Student Analytics Platform** is an independent synthetic research prototype. It is designed for learning-support analytics research, privacy-preserving workflow design, fairness auditing, and governance education.

## 1. Intended use

Acceptable uses include:

- Studying privacy-preserving student-support analytics with synthetic data.
- Teaching responsible education-data governance.
- Testing pseudonymization, access auditing, and fairness-audit workflows.
- Prototyping non-punitive support-review pipelines.
- Building reproducible synthetic-data experiments.

## 2. Non-intended use

The project is not intended for:

- Automated grading.
- Discipline or misconduct decisions.
- Admissions, scholarships, immigration, hiring, promotion, or high-stakes ranking.
- Student surveillance or behavioral policing.
- Real student intervention without educator review, student notice, appeal routes, and governance approval.
- Legal, institutional, or regulatory compliance certification.

## 3. Student-support principle

Analytics should support students, not punish them. Any support signal should be treated as a prompt for careful human review, contextual understanding, and constructive assistance.

## 4. Privacy principle

Real educational data often contains sensitive information about identity, access, socioeconomic context, disability, language, attendance, family obligations, learning challenges, and institutional support. Real deployments require a legal basis, data-protection review, minimization, retention limits, purpose limitation, and transparency to affected students.

## 5. Fairness principle

Fairness metrics are warning signals, not complete proof of justice or injustice. Subgroup differences should be interpreted with education experts, student representatives, and institutional context.

## 6. Access governance

Access to student-support analytics should be role-based, purpose-limited, logged, and periodically audited. Bulk export, after-hours access, unusual role access, and unsupported purposes should trigger human review.

## 7. Deployment boundary

This repository ships synthetic data only. Moving from this prototype to real-world deployment would require:

- Student and institutional notice.
- Approved data-governance policy.
- Privacy impact assessment.
- Bias and accessibility review.
- Human-review workflow.
- Appeal and correction mechanisms.
- Retention and deletion controls.
- External validation with representative data.
