# Methodology

This independent synthetic lab evaluates privacy-preserving student-support analytics under controlled simulation.

1. Generate fictional student profiles and weekly learning records.
2. Replace direct synthetic identifiers with salted pseudonymous IDs.
3. Audit quasi-identifier group sizes to estimate re-identification review risk.
4. Score learning-support need from aggregate engagement, attendance, completion, assessment, and help-seeking signals.
5. Audit subgroup gaps and potential over-intervention or under-support patterns.
6. Convert support signals into safe, non-punitive human-review recommendations.
7. Audit access events by role, purpose, time, and sensitivity.
8. Write reproducible outputs, figures, reports, and a hash-chained audit ledger.

The scoring models are transparent baselines for research review, not production prediction models or educational decisions.
